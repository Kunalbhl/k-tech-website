Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This policy explains how K. Tech Services collects and uses information on this website and in our products: Chalo One, Sanatan Sadhak, SocialCord Notifier and Community Manager.

What we collect

  • Details you give us, such as name and email.
  • For Discord products: your Discord user ID, username and the server settings you configure. We do not read private messages.
  • Content you create in our products, such as orders, mantra counts, applications or settings.
  • Basic technical data used to keep services secure and working.

How we use it

  • To provide and improve our products.
  • To keep accounts and communities safe.
  • To reply to your messages.

We do not sell personal data.

Service providers

We host on Google Cloud, Firebase and Cloudflare. They process data only to provide their service to us.

Your choices

Write to support@chaloone.com to access, correct or delete your data. Removing a Discord bot from your server stops all collection for that server.
