Data Deletion

SocialCord Notifier · a product of K. Tech Services · Last updated 16 November 2026

You can delete the data SocialCord Notifier holds about you at any time.

Option 1: Remove access in Instagram or Facebook (automatic)

  1. Instagram → Settings → Apps and websites → SocialCord Notifier → Remove.
  2. Facebook → Settings → Business integrations → SocialCord Notifier → Remove.

Meta tells us automatically and we delete your access token and account details.

Option 2: Ask us

email support@chaloone.com or join our Discord support server with your Instagram username, Facebook Page or Discord user ID. We confirm and delete the data within 7 days.

Discord server data

Server administrators can remove a tracked account with /remove or in the dashboard. Removing the bot from a server stops all notifications; ask us to delete that server's settings and history as well.
