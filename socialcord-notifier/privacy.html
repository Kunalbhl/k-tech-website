Privacy Policy

SocialCord Notifier · a product of K. Tech Services · Last updated 16 November 2026

SocialCord Notifier is a Discord bot and web dashboard that announces new Instagram and Facebook posts in Discord channels chosen by server administrators. It is built and operated by K. Tech Services, India ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

In short: we only read what is needed to detect new posts and deliver notifications. We never see your passwords, never post or message on your behalf, and never sell your data.

1. Information we collect

SourceWhatWhy
Discord loginDiscord user ID, username, avatar, and the list of servers you can manage (identify, guilds)To sign you in and let you configure servers you manage
Discord servers using the botServer ID and name, channel and role IDs chosen for notifications, bot settingsTo post notifications in the right place and mention the right roles
Instagram login (optional, by the account owner)Account ID, username, profile picture, account type, and the account's own public posts and collaboration posts (caption, media links, time, permalink); an access tokenTo detect new posts and show them in Discord
Facebook login (optional, by the Page admin)The Pages you choose and their linked Instagram accounts, Page posts (read only); an access tokenSame as above, for Facebook Pages
Public dataPublic posts of Instagram Business/Creator accounts that are not connected, read through Meta's official APIsTo track public accounts a server admin adds
TechnicalDelivery logs, error logs, basic request data (IP address, browser) kept for securityTo keep the service reliable and secure

Permissions requested from Meta are read-only: instagram_business_basic, instagram_business_manage_comments (required by Meta to read collaboration posts; we never write comments), pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement, instagram_basic, business_management.

2. What we never do

  • We never see or store Instagram or Facebook passwords, or your Discord password. Login happens on their own pages.
  • We never post, comment, like, or send or read direct messages on your behalf.
  • We never read the messages in your Discord server.
  • We never sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it to train AI models.

3. How we use information

Only to run SocialCord Notifier: detecting new posts, sending notifications to the Discord channels configured by the server's administrators, showing delivery history and status in the dashboard, preventing abuse, and answering support requests.

4. Sharing

We share data only with the services needed to run SocialCord Notifier: Discord and Meta (to read and deliver content), and our hosting and database providers (Google Cloud, Cloudflare, Supabase, Oracle Cloud), which process it on our behalf. We may disclose information if required by law.

5. Storage and security

Data is stored in a managed PostgreSQL database. Access tokens are encrypted at rest, all traffic uses HTTPS, and access is limited to the service itself and its operator.

6. Retention

We keep data while an account or server is using SocialCord Notifier. When access is removed or you ask us, we delete the stored tokens and account details within 7 days. Delivery and security logs are kept for up to 90 days.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Disconnect Instagram: Instagram → Settings → Apps and websites → remove SocialCord Notifier.
  • Disconnect Facebook: Facebook → Settings → Business integrations → remove SocialCord Notifier.
  • Remove from Discord: remove the bot from your server; notifications stop immediately.
  • Access, correction or deletion: contact us (below) and we will respond within 30 days. See Data Deletion.

We process personal data in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and, where applicable, other privacy laws such as the GDPR.

8. Children

SocialCord Notifier follows Discord's age requirements and is not intended for anyone under 13 (or the minimum age in their country).

9. Changes

We will update this page if our practices change and show the new date at the top.
